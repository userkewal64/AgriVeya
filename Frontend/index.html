    Data. Rules.
    Rotation.

    AgriVeya is built to be checked, not trusted blindly. The same inputs always give the same answer, and every score can be traced back to a reason.

    • Historical NASA POWER observations for your coordinates
    • A transparent weighted score, with no hidden model
    • Crop family and season checked against your last crop
    • Risks listed next to every recommendation

    What AgriVeya does not do

    • It is not a weather forecast. It shows what the climate did in the past.
    • It does not predict yield. Scores rank crops, they do not promise harvests.
    • It does not measure your soil. You tell it the soil texture.
    • Crop profiles are simplified. They are illustrative, so confirm with local advice before planting.
